Milas-Bodrum Airport (BJV)

All flights to Bodrum arrive at Milas-Bodrum Airport (BJV), about 36 km from Bodrum town. Transfer time is typically 35–50 minutes depending on traffic and resort location (Yalıkavak, Turgutreis and other peninsula resorts take longer).

Taxis, private transfers and car hire are available on arrival. Plan transfers especially for late-evening arrivals.

When to Book Flights to Bodrum

Peak demand for cheap flights to Bodrum from London is in UK school holidays, especially July and August. Shoulder periods in late spring and early autumn often offer a good balance of weather, fewer crowds and wider flight choice.
